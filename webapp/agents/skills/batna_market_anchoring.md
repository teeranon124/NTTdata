# Skill: BATNA & Market Index Benchmarking
Category: Market Intelligence & Strategic Leverage
Skill ID: `skill_batna_market`

## 1. THEORETICAL PRINCIPLE
**BATNA** (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement, Fisher & Ury) defines the buyer's walk-away threshold. Objective market benchmark indices establish credible psychological boundaries without divulging internal reservation values.

## 2. EMPIRICAL FRAMEWORK
1. **Industry Benchmark Percentiles**:
   - $P_{25}$ (`market_low`): Premier Volume Tier Rate across industrial estates (Chonburi / Rayong / Ayutthaya).
   - $P_{50}$ (`market_mid`): Median Contracted Rate.
   - $P_{75}$ (`market_high`): Single-lot Spot Buy Rate.
2. **Leverage Stance Derivation**:
   - $\text{BATNA Score} \ge 75$: High leverage (multiple qualified AVL vendors available) $\rightarrow$ Aggressive anchor citation.
   - $\text{BATNA Score} < 50$: Constrained supply / OEM sole source $\rightarrow$ Focus on lead time SLA and price stability.

## 3. AGENT EXECUTION RULES
- Cite the market range ($P_{25} - P_{75}$) and median as objective external rationale:
  *"จากการอ้างอิงดัชนีราคากลางตลาดอุตสาหกรรม ราคากลางสำหรับสัญญารายปีของชิ้นส่วนนี้อยู่ในช่วง ฿X - ฿Y ต่อหน่วย"*
- NEVER expose internal vendor bid comparison matrices or specific competitor names.
